Elastic-Potential Pathfinding

A continuous physics-based alternative to traditional grid-search algorithms.

🖱️ Drag the target (green) or obstacles (red) around to see the path adapt in real-time.

Target Attractive Force

The destination acts as a magnetic sink. It continuously pulls the terminal node, drawing the particle chain forward through open space.

Obstacles Repulsive Force

Walls and barriers exert an outward force field. As particles approach, repulsive forces push them smoothly away from geometry boundaries.

Particles Elastic Cohesion

Adjacent particles act like spring-connected links. They exert tension on each other, naturally snapping the chain taut around corner edges.
